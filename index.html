    smAisle Run

    Study the list. Route your cart. Three rounds, less time each round.

    smAisle Home ·

    How to Play

    1. 3 rounds, a new list of 8 items each. Study it in order for 17 seconds, then 12, then 8.
    2. Then the list goes away. Tap one item in every row to build your route, row 1 to row 8. Each pick opens the next row.
    3. Tap PAY. Your cart rolls the route and picks up each item in order. A wrong item tips the cart.
    4. You have 2 carts each round. Run out and the game ends. Each round cleared earns a star.
    5. Round 1: Mom texts a swap. Later rounds: a sale changes your list, or an item expires and you go back.
    6. One store a day, the same for everyone. Monday is easiest, Sunday is hardest.
